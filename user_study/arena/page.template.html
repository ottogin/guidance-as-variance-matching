SD3.5 Image Arena

Which of two images is better?

Each round shows one COCO caption and two images generated from it by Stable Diffusion 3.5 Medium. The two images come from two different generation settings, in random order, and both start from the same random noise. Pick the one you prefer overall: how good it looks, how natural it is, and how well it fits the caption. If you can't choose, call it a tie.

Keys: ← left, → right, ↓ tie, S skip. There is no fixed length: rate as many as you like and stop at any time. Every vote is saved as you go. The leaderboard opens after votes.

The study closed on . You can still rate pairs: your votes stay in this browser and make up your own leaderboard (Just me). Everyone shows the votes collected while the study was open; votes cast now are not added to it.
